Daily Itinerary

Day 1 • September 19 • Arrival & Santa Cruz

Arrive in Seville and settle into your accommodation. Spend the afternoon exploring Santa Cruz, Plaza de Santa Cruz, Calle Agua and Jardines de Murillo.

Day 2 • September 20 • Real Alcázar

🎟️ Reserved Entry: Real Alcázar at 13:00

Enjoy a relaxed morning in Santa Cruz before your Alcázar reservation. Explore the royal palace, gardens and Mudéjar courtyards. Afterwards visit Archivo de Indias and enjoy drinks overlooking the Cathedral.

📍 Real Alcázar

Day 3 • September 21 • Cathedral & Giralda

🎟️ Reserved Entry: Cathedral & Giralda at 12:40

Spend the morning exploring Santa Cruz before heading to the Cathedral. Visit Columbus' tomb and climb the Giralda Tower for panoramic views.

📍 Cathedral

Day 4 • September 22 • Casa de Pilatos, Setas & Flamenco

🎟️ Casa de Pilatos • 10:00
🎟️ Setas de Sevilla • 13:00
🎟️ Tablao Flamenco Las Setas • 19:30

Begin at Casa de Pilatos, enjoy lunch nearby, visit Setas de Sevilla for rooftop views, then spend the evening enjoying your flamenco show.

📍 Casa de Pilatos 📍 Las Setas

Day 5 • September 23 • Plaza de España & Triana

Visit Plaza de España and María Luisa Park in the morning. Cross the river to Triana for ceramics, tapas and local culture.

📍 Plaza de España 📍 Triana

Day 6 • September 24 • River Walk & Farewell

Explore Torre del Oro, the Guadalquivir River, El Arenal district and enjoy a farewell dinner.

Day 7 • September 25 • Departure

Check out and travel to Seville Airport. Recommended departure from accommodation: 07:00.

Featured Attractions

Explore Seville's most iconic attractions during your stay.


🏰 Real Alcázar
UNESCO-listed royal palace famed for its stunning Mudéjar architecture.


⛪ Seville Cathedral & Giralda
The world's largest Gothic cathedral and Seville's defining landmark.


🏛 Casa de Pilatos
One of Spain's finest examples of Andalusian palace architecture.


🍄 Setas de Sevilla
Modern architectural icon offering panoramic city views.


🌉 Triana
Historic district known for ceramics, flamenco and authentic local life.

September temperatures typically range from 26°C to 32°C. Pack lightweight clothing, sunglasses, sunscreen, comfortable shoes and a reusable water bottle.
