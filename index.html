Welcome to Seville

Discover royal palaces, Gothic cathedrals, hidden plazas, flamenco, tapas and the timeless charm of Andalusia.


Your accommodation in Santa Cruz places you within walking distance of the Real Alcázar, Seville Cathedral, the Giralda, and the UNESCO World Heritage heart of the city.

Trip Highlights

Day 1 • Arrival & Santa Cruz

Arrive at Seville Santa Justa Station and transfer to your accommodation on Calle Conde de Ibarra. Spend the evening wandering through Santa Cruz, visiting Plaza de Santa Cruz, Calle Agua, Jardines de Murillo and Plaza de Doña Elvira.


Dinner recommendations: Vineria San Telmo, El Pasaje Tapas and Bar Sal Gorda.

Day 2 • Real Alcázar & Santa Cruz

Visit the Real Alcázar, one of Europe's oldest royal palaces still in use. Explore the Mudéjar architecture, the Patio de las Doncellas and the palace gardens.


Continue to Archivo de Indias and Hospital de los Venerables before enjoying sunset drinks at the EME Catedral rooftop.

Day 3 • Cathedral & Giralda

Explore Seville Cathedral, the largest Gothic cathedral in the world, including Columbus' tomb and the famous Giralda Tower.


Continue to Iglesia del Salvador and Santa María la Blanca before evening views of the illuminated cathedral.
